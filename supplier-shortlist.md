## DIY squishy kit — NOT recommended for dropshipping as-is

On Aug 5, 2026, CPSC issued an alert about counterfeit squishy toys. It says 55 shipments were stopped at US ports, and notices of violation were sent to importers and sellers. Children's toys need third-party lab testing and a Children's Product Certificate (CPC). None of these suppliers shows any testing. They label kits "14+/18+", and some reviews mention a "strong chemical smell", which is one of CPSC's listed warning signs.
- [DIY squishy kit, 10/15/20 pcs](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256812724106921.html) (Stone's Store): $3.78–20.18 by size, 4.9★ from 25 reviews, 8–13 days. Reviewers complain about slow shipping and an unanswered refund request.
- [Crunchy chocolate squishy](https://www.aliexpress.us/item/3256813091700378.html): $7.42, 98% positive, 37 of 40 reviews from the US. Reviews mention a chemical smell.
- The **US-warehouse** squishy store (Shop1105233629: 0 years old, no feedback score) also lists a "Schylling Needoh" item. That's a likely counterfeit, so **avoid this store**.

Only go ahead if a supplier gives you CPSC-accepted lab reports (CPSIA lead/phthalates, ASTM F963) **and** you market the kit as a 14+ craft kit, not as a kids' toy.
