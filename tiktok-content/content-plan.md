# TikTok content plan: frizz-free hair account

**The idea:** grow a hair-tips account first, sell second. People follow accounts that help them; they scroll past ads. Everyone who follows a "fix your frizz" account is a likely buyer for a straightening brush later.

## 1. Set up the account (10 minutes)

- **Handle ideas:** `@frizzfixdaily`, `@thefrizzfiles`, `@smoothhairclub`. Check they're free, and claim the same name on Instagram.
- **Bio:** `Frizz-free hair tips, daily 🌿 Honest hair-tool tests 🇬🇧`
- **Account type:** start as a **personal account**. Business accounts can only use TikTok's Commercial Music Library, so you'd lose most trending sounds. Switch to a business account once your product page is live and you need the bio link. Personal accounts usually need about 1,000 followers before they can add a link.
- **Profile picture:** a simple logo or a soft hair photo. Not a product shot yet.
- Before your first post, spend 15 minutes scrolling and liking hair content (#frizzyhair, #hairtok) so TikTok learns what your account is about.

## 2. Phase 1: post these now (no product needed)

Each folder has the slides (`slide-01.png`…) and a ready-made video (`.mp4`, no sound).

**How to post:**
- **Photo mode (recommended to start):** tap **+** → **Upload** → select all the PNGs in order → TikTok makes a swipeable carousel. Carousels get lots of saves, and saves push reach.
- **Video:** upload the `.mp4` instead. Try both formats and see which your audience prefers.
- **Always add a sound in the app.** Pick something trending but quiet, at low volume. The files have no audio on purpose, because music has to come from TikTok's own library to be licensed.

## 3. Phase 2: film these when your sample arrives

These are what will actually sell the product, so they need **real footage of the real brush**. I deliberately haven't made fake or AI demos: TikTok requires AI-generated realistic content to be labelled, and in the UK misleading before/afters break advertising rules (ASA/CAP). The competitor's top critical comment was someone spotting an edited demo, so being honest is a selling point here.

Phone vertical, daylight from a window, no beauty filter on the hair.

**V1: "One take. No cuts. Real frizzy hair."** (the anchor video)
- 0–2s: frizzy hair close-up, text on screen: *"No cuts. No filters. Let's see if this thing actually works."*
- Brush one section slowly in a single continuous shot, then show the smoothed section next to an untouched one.
- End text: *"Part 2: the whole head?"* (this gets comments, and comments get reach)

**V2: "I timed it."** A phone stopwatch in the corner and full head on thick hair, real time sped up 4×. Text: *"How long does a straightening brush REALLY take?"* Say the honest final time.

**V3: "Does it actually get as hot as it says?"** Use a £10 kitchen/infrared thermometer on the bristles at each setting. Weak heat is the most common complaint about cheap brushes, so show the real numbers either way. Trust content like this builds followers fast.

**V4: "Same head, two tools."** Flat iron on one side, brush on the other. Honest verdict: sleeker vs faster and more volume. It follows on from post 4.

**V5: ASMR brushing.** No talking, close audio of brushing. In the research, an ASMR brushing video got 131K views.

**V6: "Things I wish I knew before buying a straightening brush."** Real pros and cons (works best on dry hair, use smaller sections, it isn't as sleek as a flat iron). Honesty makes the "pros" believable.

**V7: "Gift wrap with me 🎁"** Wrap the brush with a satin scrunchie and a mini hair oil. Use it in December.

**V8+: reply-to-comment videos.** Use TikTok's "reply with video" on the best question you get ("does it work on 4C hair?", "does it burn?"). These are cheap to make and very effective.

**Rules for product videos:**
- Never cut or speed up the moment the hair changes.
- No filters on hair.
- Don't claim "I've used it for years" or "salon results".
- Once the store is live, end with *"Comment BRUSH for the link"* and send it in replies/DMs.

**What "working" looks like:** the first week will probably be a few hundred views per post, which is normal for a new account. Watch **saves and shares**, not likes. A post with lots of saves is the format to make more of. Expect it to take 2–4 weeks before a post takes off.
