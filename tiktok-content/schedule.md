# Cheercrate Store: posting schedule

**Cadence:** 5 posts per 2-day block, with one batch prepared every other day.
**Times:** UK time. GMT until 29 Mar 2027, then BST; keep the same clock times.

## Where the times come from
[Sprout Social, "Best times to post on social media in the UK" (updated March 2026)](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media-uk/) analysed **25 million UK TikTok engagements**. Its windows (GMT):

| Day | Strong window (Sprout UK) | Our slots |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 4 pm – 12 am | 17:00 · 19:30 · 22:00 |
| Tue | 3 – 8 pm, 9 – 11 pm | 16:00 · 19:00 · 21:30 |
| Wed | 3 – 11 pm | 16:00 · 19:00 · 21:30 |
| Thu (**best day**) | 4 pm – 12 am | 17:00 · 20:00 · 22:30 |
| Fri | 10 – 11 pm | 22:00 · 22:40 |
| Sat (**weakest day**) | 2 – 8 pm, 10 – 11 pm | 14:30 · 17:30 · 22:15 |
| Sun | 10 – 11 pm | 22:00 · 22:40 |

**Rule for each 2-day block:** take 5 slots, giving 3 to the stronger day and 2 to the other (Thu is strongest, then Wed/Mon/Tue; Fri and Sun have only 2 slots each).

These are industry averages. Once the account has about 100 followers, check **TikTok Studio → Analytics → Followers → Follower activity** and switch to your own audience's peak hours. That's the real "verified" data for your account.
