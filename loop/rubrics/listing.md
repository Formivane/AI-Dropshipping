# Rubric: Shopify listing (node `listing`)

Sources: `supplier-shortlist.md` (supplier, plug, delivery, red flags) and the sample test notes. Never use the supplier's own marketing claims.

| Criterion | 10 | 5 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **verified** | Every spec and claim matches the sample test or the supplier evidence in the shortlist | Some untested specs, clearly marked | Untested claims (heat, magnet strength, certification) |
| **honest_delivery** | States the real delivery window (e.g. 5-8 working days from overseas) | Vague | Implies UK stock or next-day delivery |
| **clear** | Says who it's for, how to use it, what's in the box and the plug type | Missing one | Copy-paste supplier text |
| **objection** | Answers the top complaint from reviews (e.g. "takes practice", "works best on dry hair") | Hinted | Ignored |
| **compliant** | UK consumer and ASA/CAP-safe, and the returns line matches the store policy | Minor issue | Misleading |

Pass rule: `verified` = 10, `honest_delivery` = 10, all others ≥ 6.
