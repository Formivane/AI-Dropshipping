# Rubric: TikTok post (node `content`)

Sources: `tiktok-content/content-plan.md` (account idea and rules), `tiktok-content/schedule.md` (whether the product sample has arrived), and the trend-scout's evidence.

| Criterion | 10 | 5 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **hook** | Slide 1 stops the scroll in under 2 seconds: a specific pain, a number or a myth | Generic hook | No hook |
| **useful** | Someone with frizzy hair learns something they can do today | Partly useful | Filler or a disguised advert |
| **true** | Every hair-care claim is mainstream and safe (heat, protectant, satin, drying), with nothing medical | Slightly overstated | False, risky or medical |
| **save_bait** | A clear reason to save (checklist, temperatures, routine) | Weak | None |
| **honest_product** | No product mention, or a light, honest one that fits the content plan's rules. Demos only once the real sample has arrived. | Product a bit pushy | Fake demo, AI-realistic footage without a label, or a product claim nobody has tested |
| **on_series** | Fits or links to the existing series (heat guide, hair type, protectant) | Standalone | Off-topic for the account |

Pass rule: `true` = 10, `honest_product` = 10, all others ≥ 6.
